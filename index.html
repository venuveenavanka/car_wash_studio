Standard Operating Procedure

THE 6-STEP DETAILING PROCESS

Every vehicle follows our sterile multi-point studio protocol for uncompromising results.

01
Inspection

Paint depth gauge analysis and LED swirl light defect mapping.

02
Pre-Wash

pH neutral foam soak & high-pressure touchless rinse.

03
Decontam

Iron fallout chemical bath & synthetic clay bar treatment.

04
Correction

Multi-stage rotary compound polishing for 95%+ swirl elimination.

05
Protection

Infrared ceramic curing or TPU self-healing PPF installation.

06
Final QC

Hex-light quality assurance check and handover certificate.
